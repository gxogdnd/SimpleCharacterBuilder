# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This is a learning project. People will arrive with very different levels of
experience, and that is exactly what we want. We pledge to make taking part a
welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps everyone learn:

- Being patient and kind, especially with beginners.
- Assuming good intent and asking clarifying questions.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Remembering that "I don't understand this yet" is a normal and welcome thing
  to say.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments of any kind.
- Dismissing or belittling questions because they seem basic.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any other project communication.

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please report it to the
maintainer at **gxogdnd@gmail.com**. Reports will be handled discreetly. The
maintainer may take any action they deem appropriate, up to and including a
permanent ban from the project.
